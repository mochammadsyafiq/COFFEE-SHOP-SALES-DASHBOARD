## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify revenue trends, profitability, product performance, and customer payment preferences.

An interactive dashboard was developed to transform transaction data into meaningful business insights through KPI cards, data visualizations, Pivot Tables, and interactive slicers.

The dashboard allows users to explore sales performance by month, product category, customer type, and payment method, supporting more efficient reporting and data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Evaluate sales performance across product categories.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Identify top-performing products based on revenue.
- Analyze sales contributions across Member, Non-Member, and VIP customer segments.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for sales monitoring and business reporting.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers that allow users to filter and explore the data based on:

- **Month-Year** — analyze sales performance over a selected period.
- **Product Category** — compare revenue across product categories.
- **Customer Type** — explore sales contributions from different customer segments.
- **Payment Method** — examine transaction patterns by payment method.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard presents four key metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from transactions |
| Total Profit | Total profit generated from sales |
| Profit Margin (%) | Percentage of sales retained as profit |
| Number of Transactions | Total number of recorded transactions |

### 3. Main Visualizations

**Monthly Revenue & Profit Trends**

A line or area chart illustrating monthly revenue and profit movements to identify sales patterns and high- or low-performing periods.

**Revenue by Product Category**

A bar chart comparing revenue across product categories, such as Milk-Based, Espresso-Based, and Cold Brew products, to identify major revenue contributors.

**Payment Method Distribution**

A doughnut chart showing the distribution of transactions across payment methods, including Cash, QRIS, E-Wallet, Debit Card, and Credit Card.

**Top Products by Revenue**

A horizontal bar chart highlighting the highest-revenue products, with a breakdown by customer type to explore differences in purchasing contributions.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the analysis of 20,000 transaction records, the following findings were identified:

- **Total Sales:** Approximately 2.09 billion in the dataset's currency units.
- **Total Profit:** Approximately 712.95 million in the dataset's currency units.
- **Profit Margin:** Approximately 34.11%, based on total profit divided by total sales.
- **Top Revenue Category:** Manual Brew generated approximately 530.43 million in sales in the analyzed category summary.
- **Monthly Sales Performance:** May recorded approximately 181.72 million in revenue, while April recorded approximately 157.57 million.
- **Payment Preferences:** QRIS recorded 4,073 transactions, making it the most frequently used payment method in the analyzed data.
- **Top Product:** Macchiato generated approximately 157.38 million in revenue in the product summary.

These findings provide an overview of sales performance, profitability, and customer transaction behavior. The interactive dashboard enables further exploration of these patterns using the available filters.
